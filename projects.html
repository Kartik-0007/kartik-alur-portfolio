Online Harassment Detection System

In Progress

An NLP-based project aimed at identifying potentially threatening, abusive, insulting, or harassing messages in online communication and flagging them for human review or moderation.

Project Objective

Explore a system that can analyze text, recognize harmful language patterns, and support safer online interactions through moderation assistance.

Planned Development

  • Prepare and clean a suitable labelled text dataset.
  • Explore text representation and classification approaches.
  • Evaluate the detection workflow and flag potentially harmful messages for review.

This project is currently in progress. Implementation details and results will be updated as development continues.
